Economists agree on more than you think.

Across 1,119 IGM Forum survey questions: 53% reach a confident answer (HHI ≥ 0.5), and 23% reach overwhelming consensus (HHI ≥ 0.75). Only 22% are genuinely contested — including the public fights you've heard of (AI risks, kidney markets, the $15 minimum wage).

Data: Kent A. Clark Center for Global Markets (IGM Forum), 558 surveys 2011–2026. Direction-blind consensus metric: HHI = share_agree² + share_uncertain² + share_disagree² (1.0 = unanimous; 0.33 = perfectly diffuse three-way split). Hover any column for the question; click opens the source poll.
